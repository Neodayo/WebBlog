Our tour kicked off with a cultural immersion day in the heart of Manila. We explored Rizal Park, paying tribute to the national hero José Rizal, and wandered through the historic walled city of Intramuros — a living testament to Philippine colonial history. This day set the tone for the week: curiosity, discovery, and perspective.

Day three brought us to two very different organizations. At the MMDA (Metro Manila Development Authority), we witnessed how technology drives urban traffic management and public safety across the metro. Then at Teleperformance, one of the world's largest BPO companies, we explored the intersection of IT, customer experience, and large-scale operations.
🚦 Traffic Systems 📡 Command Center 🎧 BPO Operations 🌐 Global Scale 📱 Smart City
